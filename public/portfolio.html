// about me

Who I Am

I'm Balaji Nayak, a passionate full-stack developer and tech entrepreneur based in India. I founded Balanju Solutions with a vision to build software that makes developers' and businesses' lives easier.

My journey in software development started with a curiosity for how things work. Today, I build everything from online compilers and web applications to developer tools, always with a focus on clean code, great UX, and performance.

At Balanju Solutions, we believe technology should be accessible to everyone. That's why flagship products like Our Compiler are completely free, with no sign-up required — just open your browser and start coding.

When I'm not coding, I'm exploring new technologies, contributing to open source, and thinking about the next product that can make a difference.
