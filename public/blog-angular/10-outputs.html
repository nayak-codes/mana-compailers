11Common Mistakes & Anti-Patterns
⚠️ Anti-Patterns to Avoid
  • Forgetting to unsubscribe from manual RxJS Observables, leading to memory leaks.
  • Omitting track in @for loops causing full list DOM re-renders on array updates.
  • Mutating objects directly instead of updating Signals via .set() or .update().
  • Putting heavy computation directly inside HTML template expression interpolation.
  • Injecting services in non-injection contexts instead of constructors or inject() functions.
