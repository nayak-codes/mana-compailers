Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Our Compiler (operated by Balanju Solutions) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and online code compiler.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Our Compiler ("we," "us," or "our"), operated by Balanju Solutions. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you visit ourcompiler.com and use our free online code compiler and programming tutorials.

By using Our Compiler, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the website.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration. You can use the compiler and tutorials without providing your name, email, or any personally identifiable information.

We may automatically collect limited technical information when you visit our site, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website or search query
  • IP address (used for security and approximate location)

This data helps us maintain site performance, prevent abuse, and improve user experience. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

3. Code Execution & Submissions

When you click "Run Code," your source code and any stdin input are transmitted securely via HTTPS to our backend execution service for compilation and running.

  • Code is processed in memory only and is not permanently stored on our servers after execution completes.
  • We do not use your code to train AI models or share it with third parties.
  • Execution logs may be temporarily retained for debugging and abuse prevention, then automatically deleted.
  • Do not submit passwords, API keys, personal data, or confidential information in code you run on the platform.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Browser localStorage: We use your browser's local storage to save your editor code drafts, theme preference (dark/light mode), and template version — all stored locally on your device, not on our servers.

Cookies: Our website may use cookies for essential functionality and, where applicable, for advertising purposes (see Section 5). You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

5. Advertising (Google AdSense)

To keep Our Compiler free, we use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other sites on the Internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, visit aboutads.info to opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized ads.

For more information on how Google uses data, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Our Compiler:

  • Google AdSense — advertising (see Section 5)
  • Google Fonts — web typography delivery
  • Cloud hosting providers — website and code execution infrastructure

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites linked from our tutorials.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit, Docker container isolation for code execution, execution timeouts, and resource limits to prevent abuse. While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

8. Data Retention

Source code submitted for execution is not retained after the run completes. Technical server logs may be kept for up to 30 days for security and debugging purposes. Local storage data remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall your browser data.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising via browser or Google settings
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Compiler is intended for general audiences including students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete such information.

11. International Users

Our Compiler is operated from India and is accessible worldwide. By using our service, you consent to the processing of information in accordance with this policy. If you are accessing from the European Economic Area (EEA) or other regions with data protection laws, you retain the rights described in Section 9.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
