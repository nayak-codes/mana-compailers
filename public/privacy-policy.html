Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026. Learn about what we collect, how we run your code, and our zero-tracking design.

1. Overview

Our Compiler is built as a zero-login, free-to-use playground. We value your privacy. This privacy page details our policy regarding user inputs, code submissions, data tracking, and external vendors.

2. What We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information. You do not need to register an account or verify your identity to run code. The compiler works directly using sandbox nodes.

To compile your scripts, we must send your code lines and inputs to the backend Docker compiler service. This data is handled in memory and discarded immediately once execution is completed.

3. Code & Submission Policy

When you run your program, we apply the following guidelines:

  • No code submissions are cached, saved, or logged permanently on our storage servers.
  • We do not share your code files, custom algorithms, or private designs with third-party networks.
  • Your code lines are never used to train machine learning tools or analytics networks.

4. Cookies & Web Cache

We use localized browser storage (localStorage) to hold your active code templates, theme preferences, and editor settings. This data resides solely on your machine. We do not sync this state to our servers.

5. Google AdSense & Third Parties

To keep this compiler free for everyone, the platform may include ad banners served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies and device identifiers to serve tailored ads based on your site visits. You can manage or disable cookie behavior in your browser settings.

For more information, please read the Google Privacy Rules & Terms.

6. Security Measures

All user submissions go through secure HTTPS routing to prevent middleman sniffing. Additionally, all execution scripts execute inside quarantined Docker containers, preventing execution loops or memory leak exploits from affecting general stability.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Compiler is a developer utility designed for hobbyists and students. We do not gather or track any information from children under 13. If you believe your child has submitted sensitive data, contact us for prompt support.

8. Updates

We may update this policy periodically to align with runtime modifications or AdSense updates. The last updated timestamp is always shown at the top of the section.
